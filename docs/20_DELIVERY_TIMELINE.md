# Delivery timeline and expansion

## Baseline
One full-time Cursor developer. Working days are relative to the agreed implementation start; holidays, access delays and scope can shift dates. The earlier 16-29 October internal platform launch is superseded. Planning documents prepared today do not prove discovery decisions or release gates have passed.

| Working days | Work | Demonstration / gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-2 | Discovery, pilot selection, code audit, providers and architecture | Agreed scope, access and decision log |
| 3-5 | Repo, staging, database, registry, permissions and starter | Two site records and reproducible staging |
| 6-7 | SEO baseline, templates and form contract | Reusable foundation demo, endpoint validation |
| 8-10 | Durable lead storage, outbox, email and protected admin | End-to-end enquiry plus failed-email demonstration |
| 11-13 | Booking, analytics and conversion checks | Correct booking modes, no PII or double-counting |
| 14-15 | Second-site dry run and cross-client/browser tests | Functional pilot and real backend reuse |
| 16-18 | Independent review, monitoring, backups, restore and incident guide | Critical/high findings resolved, recovery evidence |
| 19-20 | Controlled release, verification, fixes and handover | G1-G5 passed or documented launch block |

Milestones: foundation in 5-7 days; functional staging pilot in 10-15; controlled launch target by 20. Do not cut testing to preserve a date. Re-estimate complex booking or Wix migration before committing.

## Later phases
| Phase | Indicative window | Entry / result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 Agency operations | Weeks 5-7 | Stable Phase 1 and maintenance owner; original ten features except consolidated reporting |
| 3 Multi-site/reporting | Weeks 8-12 | Second-site reuse proven; 2-3 real sites, GA4/GSC consolidation and monthly reporting |
| 4 Intelligence | Subsequent 4-8+ weeks | Verified sources; audits, opportunities, alerts and controlled AI observation |
| 5 Advanced platform | Demand-led | Justified CMS, booking adapters, off-site modules and broader automation |

Every expansion includes migration safety, regression checks, documentation and recovery. Public pages and enquiries must remain operational independently of agency dashboards and intelligence features.
