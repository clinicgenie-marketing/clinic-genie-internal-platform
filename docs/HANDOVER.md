# Handover

Prepared 19 October 2026 at the end of the first build session. Read `docs/BUILD_STATUS.md` first on a resumed session.

## 1. What works and how to open it

See `README.md` for the five commands. Open `http://localhost:3400`, sign in as `admin@example.invalid` through the development links page, and you land on the dashboard.

Implemented and exercised in the browser:

- Sign-in (development magic link, Google Workspace ready), server-side access resolution, sign-out.
- Dashboard: urgent, due today, waiting and upcoming sections from the deterministic queue score; approvals waiting; issues needing verification; recent timeline. Quick actions save and refresh.
- Clients: list with task counts, profile (owner, channels, notes, archive and restore), websites, account references with a credential guard, staff access grants, and tabs for every module scoped to the client.
- Tasks: create, edit, one accountable owner, priority rules, waiting-on and follow-up, status history, notes, client-scoped related-record selector.
- Approvals: draft, submit, withdraw, append-only decisions with supersession and full history.
- SEO: page mapping with keywords, intent, content status, review dates, manual mapping flags, before and after change records with verification.
- Google Ads: audits with review periods, scoped findings and recommendations, change log with before and after values, reason, responsible staff, approval reference, verification and outcome.
- Issues: open, investigating, resolved pending verification, verified closed, reopened; verifier, date and result; evidence as private uploads (served only to authorised staff) or https links.
- Medical review: claims, wording versions, reviewer assignment, sources, immutable decisions, earlier reviews preserved on wording change, no approval without a source.
- Reports: immutable snapshots, supersession on correction, like-for-like comparison with zero-baseline and unavailable handling, observations kept apart from explanations.
- Timeline: generated by the workflows in the same transaction, idempotent, separate from the audit log.
- Admin: status, users and roles, audit history, backup and restore records.

## 2. Setup and environment

`README.md` lists the variable names. Secrets go in `.env` locally and in the host's environment settings elsewhere. The embedded database is for development; staging and production need a managed PostgreSQL (`DATABASE_URL` and `DATABASE_ADMIN_URL`), then `npm run db:migrate` and, for demonstrations only, `npm run db:seed`.

## 3. Demonstration journey (about ten minutes)

1. Sign in as `admin@example.invalid`. The dashboard shows four urgent tasks, two due today and two waiting. Click Complete on "Update implant page meta description" and watch it leave the queue.
2. Open Clients, then Harbourview Dental. The overview shows priority tasks, recent activity, websites and account references (placeholders, with vault references instead of passwords).
3. Open the Approvals tab. "Rewrite implant page title and description" is submitted; approve it with a note. Open "Pause Display campaign" under Northgate Physio to see a revised decision with the superseded one kept.
4. Open SEO. The Meridian pages are flagged as duplicate target and cannibalisation. Open `/dental-implants` to see a verified title change with the observed movement recorded as observation, not cause.
5. Open Google Ads, then "September search audit": three findings, one implemented through a logged change with an approval reference and a verification note. The audit is closed.
6. Open Issues. "Contact form emails not reaching reception" is resolved pending verification with an evidence link. Verify it as the admin (the resolver cannot verify their own fix unless they are an admin).
7. Open Medical review. "Painless dental implants" was rejected. "Acne treatment clears acne in two weeks" had changes required, the wording was revised, and the earlier review is preserved as superseded. Try to approve a claim without a source: it is refused.
8. Open Reports, choose Harbourview Dental. The 28-day periods compare like-for-like; confirmed appointments show "zero baseline, no %"; patients show "previous unavailable"; the corrected enquiries figure shows as a correction with the earlier value kept. Switch to the calendar months to see the "not like-for-like" warning.
9. Open Timeline and filter by client or event type. Open Admin for the backup and restore records.
10. Sign out and sign in as `specialist-b@example.invalid`: only Northgate Physio is visible, and direct URLs for other clients return not found.
