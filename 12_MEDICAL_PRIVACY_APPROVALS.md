# Medical, privacy and release approvals

This file implements the supplied plan's process requirements. It is not legal clearance or a substitute for current clinical/regulatory review. Referenced Clinic Genie frameworks must be supplied and versioned before claiming conformance; their contents were not included in the source PDF.

## Before live personal information
Record clinic/Clinic Genie responsibilities, purposes, authorised users, processing locations, provider agreements, retention, deletion/export, incident response and cross-border arrangements. Use minimal contact/request data. Exclude medical history, NRIC and clinical documents unless separately scoped and reviewed.

Privacy notices and optional marketing consent must be approved for the actual form and purpose. Separate optional marketing choice from essential enquiry handling. Record required acknowledgement/consent evidence according to the approved policy. Do not invent a blanket consent rule.

## Medical copy
Keep approved content version, source references, reviewer identity, date and outcome with release evidence. Separate medical/factual approval from marketing approval. Changes to clinical meaning require renewed review. An AI check or checkbox alone cannot authorise patient-facing medical claims.

## Accountability
Name platform owner, clinic/medical approver, independent qualified security reviewer, developer, maintenance owner and incident primary/backup. Maintain a decision log. Unknown approvals are blockers, not implied consent.

## Acceptance
Production content matches the approved version. Form collection matches approved fields. Review evidence is inspectable. Logs/analytics contain no raw patient details. No live enquiry launch before the security, recovery and clinic approval gates pass. Full approvals inbox and medical-review workflow remain Phase 2.
